## 👋 About me

Hey, I'm **Musteqeem**, also known as **Future Scientist** — a **full-stack app and website developer**, **ethical hacker**, and the **CEO of CyberShield Squad**.

I enjoy bringing ideas to life across the stack: crafting web experiences and apps, building bot and AI projects, and exploring security with a responsible, authorized mindset. I care about making technology useful, polished, and safer to use.
